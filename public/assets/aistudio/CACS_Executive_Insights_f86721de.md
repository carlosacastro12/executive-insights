Development research · Aqu.ai

### A two-level hydraulic and edge-AI farming system

Aqu.ai is a working prototype for low-income rural farms in drought-hit New Mexico and runoff-heavy Louisiana. Water, soil and minerals percolate from the top level to the bottom through natural filtration and solar recirculation.

Growing units

6,000

60 × 50 buckets on each of two levels

Local AI model

\<4B

Parameters, running offline on the farm

Target regions

2

New Mexico and Louisiana

Connectivity needed

None

No broadband, no cloud subscription

Subsystem 1

### Two-level gravity hydraulic array

Captures percolating soil and mineral leachate between levels and tests flow rates and root-zone hydration.

Subsystem 2

### Off-grid green infrastructure

Solar-DC micro-pumping with bio-char, sand and gravel filtration, built from low-embodied-energy parts.

Subsystem 3

### Offline-first edge AI co-pilot

Turns water and soil telemetry into plain-language decisions that the farmer controls.

New Mexico

Megadroughts, depleted aquifers and Acequia water-sharing limits make closed-loop reuse and nutrient retention essential.

Louisiana

Intense rain, nutrient leaching and runoff make on-site water capture, mineral retention and natural filtration vital.

Issue, solution, improvement

### Five questions the data raises

Why do 1,911 impressions produce one reaction and no comments?

Issue

Posts announce. They do not ask anything, so readers click and leave.

Solution

End every post with one specific question. Reply within the hour. Tag the partners mentioned.

Improve

December target: five reactions and one comment per post.

Which format earns attention?

Issue

The two "connect. create. collaborate." image posts earned zero clicks and 0% engagement.

Solution

Lead with an article that names a real business issue, then follow with a video that gives the solution.

Improve

Move the mix from 33% to 60% articles and video with a question attached.

Why does reach stall near 100 per post?

Issue

The follower base is small. The best post reached 140 impressions.

Solution

Invite the 330 email contacts to follow the page and ask partners to reshare.

Improve

Boost the top two posts each month.

Is the posting rhythm right?

Issue

Three posts went out on Oct 28 and the next days fell quiet.

Solution

Publish three times a week on fixed days.

Improve

Move from 1.9 to 3 posts a week.

Does Spanish content work?

Issue

Unclear whether bilingual posts pay off.

Solution

Spanish videos on Oct 31 and Nov 11 held 9.1% and 11.8% engagement. Keep them and pair each with English.

Improve

One bilingual pair per solution.
